KEYS

 ?  F1        these pages
 TAB  F2      show or hide the move hints
 ^S  F3       sound on or off
 1-6          netrun an exploit from your kit
 A-L, 0-8     name a node, then ENTER
 ENTER        confirm
 BACKSPACE    undo
 UP  DOWN     step back through the log
 < >  <- ->   turn these pages
 ESC          close these pages; on the title,
              quit (then Y)

Colour needs a terminal with 256 colours and
UTF-8, at least 80 columns by 24 rows.
