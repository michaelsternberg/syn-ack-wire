**SYN-ACK WIRE** v0.7.0 brings the game to the Coleco Adam.

**Adam** (with a FujiNet for the Adam):
- The NES's screen on the Adam's 32 columns, in colour: the Grid and the seats on green panels, the bottom lines
  magenta while the decision is yours.
- Played with the keyboard, as on the other computers: SmartKey I opens the instructions, II the move hints, III
  the sound. Or with the controller, as on the NES: a yellow cursor on the answer the left fire button would give,
  a box on the node an exploit would spike, the handle entered with a letter dial. Or both at once.
- The Atari's title logo, its letters filled with TV static; the Atari's chords.
- Adam players meet Atari, Apple II, PC, CoCo, NES and H89 players at the same tables.

Pick a SYN-ACK WIRE table in the FujiNet game lobby, or mount `syn-ack-wire-adam.ddp` from
`syn-ack-wire.fly.dev` in FujiNet's CONFIG and boot it.

**Atari, Apple II, MS-DOS, CoCo, NES and H89:** unchanged. Boot `syn-ack-wire-atari.atr`, `syn-ack-wire-apple2.po`,
`syn-ack-wire-msdos.img`, `syn-ack-wire-coco.dsk` or `syn-ack-wire-nes.nes` from FujiNet, or pick any table from
the FujiNet game lobby (the H89: mount `syn-ack-wire-h89.h8d` in CP/M). The PDFs of the complete manuals, the Adam's now among them, are included below.
