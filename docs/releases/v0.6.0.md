**SYN-ACK WIRE** v0.6.0 brings the game to the Nintendo Entertainment System.

**NES** (with a FujiNet NES cartridge):
- The screen laid out anew in 30 columns, inside a TV's edges, in colour: the Grid and the seats on green
  panels, the bottom lines purple while the decision is yours.
- Played on the pad: a yellow cursor on the answer A would give, a box on the node an exploit would spike, the
  handle entered with a letter dial. SELECT opens the instructions, SELECT+A the move hints, SELECT+B the sound.
- The Atari's title logo, its letters filled with TV static; the Atari's chords.
- NES players meet Atari, Apple II, PC and CoCo players at the same tables.

Pick a SYN-ACK WIRE table in the FujiNet game lobby, or mount `syn-ack-wire-nes.nes` from
`syn-ack-wire.fly.dev` in FujiNet's CONFIG and boot it.

**Atari, Apple II, MS-DOS and CoCo:** unchanged. Boot `syn-ack-wire-atari.atr`, `syn-ack-wire-apple2.po`,
`syn-ack-wire-msdos.img` or `syn-ack-wire-coco.dsk` from FujiNet, or pick any table from the FujiNet game lobby.
