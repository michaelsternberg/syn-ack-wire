**SYN-ACK WIRE** v0.4.0 brings 40 columns, colour and a steadier connection to MS-DOS.

**MS-DOS:**
- **40 or 80 columns.** 40 columns read clearly on a TV or a composite monitor. The game asks at start (press 1
  or 2); `SET SYNACK=40` or `SET SYNACK=80` in AUTOEXEC.BAT skips the question, and F4 on the title screen swaps.
- **Colour.** On a colour display, F5 turns on green phosphor: each syndicate in its own colour, your questions
  on magenta. It starts off, as some laptop LCDs show colour poorly.
- **Quit to DOS.** Esc on the title screen, then Y.
- **A steadier connection.** A moment's glitch on the serial line no longer drops the connection, which could
  cost you a turn while the game redialled.
- The circuit board either side shows on grey LCD screens too.

Pick a SYN-ACK WIRE table in the FujiNet game lobby, or put `syn-ack-wire-msdos.img` in a FujiNet slot, mount it
with `FMOUNT`, and type `SYNACK` on its drive.

**All platforms:** the table you pick in the FujiNet lobby reaches the game through FujiNet's SD card. Without
one, the game joins the public server's CHIBA-GRID-7 table. The manuals now say so.

**Apple II and Atari:** boot `syn-ack-wire-apple2.po` or `syn-ack-wire-atari.atr` from FujiNet, or pick any table
from the FujiNet game lobby. The PDFs of the complete manuals are included below.
