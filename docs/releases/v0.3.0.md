**SYN-ACK WIRE** v0.3.0 brings the game to MS-DOS. PC, Apple II and Atari players can compete at the same table.

**MS-DOS (new):** for the IBM PC and compatibles, from the original 4.77 MHz 8088 up, with an RS-232 FujiNet and
its `FUJINET.SYS` driver. It runs on any display adapter, monochrome or colour, and plays its sounds on an AdLib or
Sound Blaster when there is one, else on the PC speaker. Put `syn-ack-wire-msdos.img` in a FujiNet slot, mount it
with `FMOUNT`, and type `SYNACK` on its drive: it joins the public server's CHIBA-GRID-7 table. The game has
built-in help, with a QR code to the manual. The PDF of the complete manual is included below.

**Apple II and Atari:** boot `syn-ack-wire-apple2.po` or `syn-ack-wire-atari.atr` from FujiNet, or pick any table
from the FujiNet game lobby.
