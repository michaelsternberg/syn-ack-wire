**SYN-ACK WIRE** v0.5.0 brings the game to the TRS-80 Color Computer.

**CoCo 1 and 2** (32K or more, with a FujiNet for the CoCo):
- 40 columns on the high-resolution screen, in green and black; the up arrow swaps to black on green.
- The Atari's title logo, its letters filled with TV static.
- CLEAR turns the move hints on or off, SHIFT+CLEAR the sound, and ? opens the instructions, any time.
- CoCo players meet Atari, Apple II and PC players at the same tables.

Pick a SYN-ACK WIRE table in the FujiNet game lobby, or mount `syn-ack-wire-coco.dsk` in drive 0 in FujiNet's
CONFIG and boot: the game starts by itself.

**Atari, Apple II and MS-DOS:** unchanged. Boot `syn-ack-wire-atari.atr`, `syn-ack-wire-apple2.po` or
`syn-ack-wire-msdos.img` from FujiNet, or pick any table from the FujiNet game lobby. The PDFs of the complete
manuals, the CoCo's now among them, are included below.

**No vintage machine?** Play at the same tables on Windows, macOS or Linux: see the
[desktop releases](https://github.com/michaelsternberg/syn-ack-wire/releases?q=desktop&expanded=true).
