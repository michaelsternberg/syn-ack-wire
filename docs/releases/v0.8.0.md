**SYN-ACK WIRE** v0.8.0 brings the game to the Intellivision.

**Intellivision** (with a FujiNet Intellivision cartridge):
- The game on the Intellivision's 20 by 12 cards, in three views the disc steps through: the Grid, each hive in its
  syndicate's colour; the seats and the log; and who holds what crypto. The question and the shot clock stay at
  the bottom of every view, purple while the decision is yours.
- Played on the keypad: 1-6 run an exploit, 1-7 pick a syndicate by its line beside the Grid, digits and ENTER
  for a hivejack's crypto, CLEAR to undo. The top buttons open the instructions, the lower left the options (move
  hints, sound, log off), the lower right the next view. The handle is dialled with the disc.
- An ECS keyboard, if one is attached, types as on the other computers.
- The Atari's title logo in colored squares, its letters filled with TV static; the Atari's chords on the PSG.
- Intellivision players meet Atari, Apple II, PC, CoCo, NES and Adam players at the same tables.

Pick a SYN-ACK WIRE table in the FujiNet game lobby, or mount `syn-ack-wire-intv.rom` from
`syn-ack-wire.fly.dev` in FujiNet's CONFIG and boot it.

**Atari, Apple II, MS-DOS, CoCo, NES and Adam:** unchanged. Boot `syn-ack-wire-atari.atr`,
`syn-ack-wire-apple2.po`, `syn-ack-wire-msdos.img`, `syn-ack-wire-coco.dsk`, `syn-ack-wire-nes.nes` or
`syn-ack-wire-adam.ddp` from FujiNet, or pick any table from the FujiNet game lobby. The PDFs of the complete
manuals, the Intellivision's now among them, are included below.
